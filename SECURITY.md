# Security Policy

GitWallet is a **non-custodial** wallet: your seed phrase and private keys never leave your device and are never sent to any server. Security is our top priority.

## Reporting a vulnerability

If you discover a security issue, please report it **privately** — do not open a public issue.

- Email: **gitwallet.official@gmail.com**
- Please include steps to reproduce, affected versions, and any proof of concept.

We aim to acknowledge reports within **72 hours** and will keep you updated on the fix. Responsible disclosure is appreciated — give us reasonable time to patch before any public disclosure.

## Good to know

- GitWallet never asks for your secret phrase or private keys. **No one from GitWallet will ever ask for them.**
- The app has no account and no password recovery — your secret phrase is the only backup. Keep it offline and private.
- Always download the app from an official source: [gitwallet.net](https://gitwallet.net) or [APKPure](https://apkpure.com/p/com.gitwallet.app).
