# Install guide (Android)

GitWallet is not on Google Play yet, so you install it from the `.apk` file. Android shows a normal **Play Protect** notice — this is expected and safe. The app's code is the same.

## Steps

1. **Download the app**
   - From [APKPure](https://apkpure.com/p/com.gitwallet.app), or
   - From [gitwallet.net](https://gitwallet.net) (button or QR code).

2. **Open the downloaded `.apk`**
   Your browser or file manager will ask to open the installer file.

3. **Play Protect → "More"**
   Play Protect may say the app wasn't scanned. This is a normal warning, not an error. Tap **More**.

4. **"Install anyway"**
   Tap **Install anyway**. The app installs.

That's it — open GitWallet and create a new wallet in a minute, or import an existing one with its secret phrase.

> The warnings disappear once the app is published on Google Play. Your keys and secret phrase always stay on your phone.

## Requirements
- Android 8.0 or newer
- ~150 MB free space
