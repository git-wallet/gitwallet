# Features

## One wallet, 14 blockchains
A single secret phrase opens Bitcoin, Ethereum, BNB Chain, Solana, Tron, Polygon, Arbitrum, Optimism, Base, Avalanche, Litecoin, Dogecoin, XRP Ledger and Cosmos. Send, receive and store coins, tokens and NFTs in one place.

## Non-custodial by design
Your seed phrase and private keys are generated and stored **only on your device**, encrypted at rest and protected by a PIN and/or biometrics. Nothing is uploaded to a server. No sign-up, no email, no KYC.

## Swaps & exchange
Trade assets across chains directly inside the app, with a clear view of price, slippage and fees.

## NFT gallery
Browse and manage your NFTs alongside your tokens.

## Futures on Hyperliquid
Open long/short positions with on-screen entry, mark price and unrealized PnL.

## Voice AI assistant
Ask in plain language — *"send 10 USDT to …"* or *"what did I spend on fees this week?"*. The assistant reads balances, addresses and recent activity and **prepares** the action. You always review and sign it yourself. Four AI models to choose from; Gemini is free.

## Trading bot powered by Claude
Automated strategies with hard limits and a **practice mode** (no real money until you're ready). Connect up to 10 exchanges. Runs by your rules.

## Token builder
Create your own token in minutes, right from the app.

## Safety
- Address checks warn you about suspicious or scam addresses before you send.
- Transaction previews in plain words.

## Localized
16 interface languages.
