# FAQ

### Is GitWallet custodial? Does it hold my funds?
No. GitWallet is **non-custodial**. Your seed phrase and private keys are generated and stored only on your device. GitWallet never holds your funds and cannot access, freeze or move them.

### Do I need an account or email?
No. There's no sign-up, no email and no KYC. Your secret phrase is your wallet.

### What happens if I lose my phone?
Restore your wallet on a new device using your secret phrase. **This is the only backup** — write it down and keep it offline and private. No one can recover it for you.

### Will anyone from GitWallet ask for my secret phrase?
Never. Anyone asking for your secret phrase or private keys is trying to scam you.

### Why does Android warn me when installing?
The app isn't on Google Play yet, so Play Protect shows a standard notice. It's safe — see the [install guide](INSTALL.md).

### How many blockchains are supported?
14, from one seed phrase. See [NETWORKS.md](NETWORKS.md).

### Is the trading bot safe to use?
The bot has hard limits and a **practice mode** so you can test strategies with no real money until you're ready. You stay in control.

### How much does it cost?
The app is free to download. On-chain transactions pay the usual network fees.

### Which AI models does the assistant use?
You can choose among four models; Gemini is available for free.
